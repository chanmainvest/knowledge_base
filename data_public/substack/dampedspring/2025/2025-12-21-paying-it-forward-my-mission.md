# Paying it forward. My mission.

- Publication: dampedspring (dampedspring)
- URL: https://dampedspring101.substack.com/p/paying-it-forward-my-mission
- Published: 2025-12-21
- Audience: everyone

Exciting news for dampedspring.com paid subscribers. THIS SUBSTACK Will always be free. But DSRead/Alpha/Institutional is getting even better in 2026.

I have spent almost 40 years thinking about, researching, and trading markets. Due to hard work, good luck, and perhaps some above average aptitude I describe my life as having been a success so far. Perhaps oddly, I remain just as driven to improve my understanding of markets today as ever.

As I look across my whole career the motivation that defines my life has been paying my knowledge and experience forward. Figuring stuff out has always driven me. I love to solve puzzles and markets are a constant challenge. But perhaps more importantly once figuring something out I have always been highly motivated to share what I have learned. I suspect my desire to teach comes from my background. My uncle was a renowned professor at UPenn, My mother is a life long professional teacher, my wife and daughter are teachers. I really value teaching.

I started my career in 1986. Within a year I was figuring stuff out and teaching what I learned to others. **Literally anyone who has ever worked with me knows my true passion is paying my understandings forward.** I have been teaching others what I know for 40 years and will continue for years to come.

The common wisdom in markets is if you figure something out it must be kept secret and exploited for profit. For 40 years I have struggled with this concept, probably costing me some alpha. At the same time I have found that teaching others what you know is also a super power. The biggest challenge for any investor or trader is to convince someone to provide you the risk capital to invest in strategies you figure out. Throughout my career explaining what I wanted to do was a necessity. For example, at Salomon Brothers I noticed massive opportunity to

* Arbitrage convertible bonds
* Take advantage of the explosion of credit markets caused by the Drexel Bankruptcy and Savings and Loan crisis
* Arbitrage capital structures across credit, equity, and equity options markets
* Better implement risk arbitrage trades via credit and equity options strategies
* Develop high frequency trading strategies to make standalone alpha and to use computers to market make.
* Arbitrage single name options against index options and arbitrage skew and term structure of volatility surfaces
* Take advantage of forced unwinds of leveraged investors
* Create corporate products to optimize share repurchases and issuance
* Create high net worth products to provide hedges and financing to holders of concentrated single names
* Arbitrage global volatility markets
* Take advantage of inelastic flows due to structural benchmark trading

Let me tell you when you see these opportunities the trick isn’t finding the opportunity it is explaining to a wide cohort of bosses and peers what you need from them and why its a good idea to commit time and capital to the opportunity. Perhaps the greatest skill I have is doing that explaining in a credible way. Credible consistency is built on being assertive but also being openminded and honest about what you don’t know. **I have hundreds of specific examples of teaching others my trades that I want to pay forward.**

Another common wisdom is a quote from George Bernard Shaw

**"Those who can, do; those who can't, teach".**

I’ve spent 40 years teaching while also doing. I think that Richard Feynman said it better.

**“If you want to master something, teach it**"

I get why people want to keep information to themselves, Trust me I’ve been at some firms with some pretty nutso intellectual property protection schemes. Furthermore I’ve seen many firms succeed in raising money with no transparency about what they are doing essentially saying “we have a black box of alpha and we won’t tell you what we do but its good so give us money”. There are lots of ways to skin a cat in investing. I just have to do it my way as I can’t do it any other way. I do, I teach, I learn by teaching and as a consequence I give up some value to others that I could perhaps have greedily kept for myself. Net net I think my method is better than the alternative but respect others methods as well.

So given my goal to pay forward my understandings I have devoted most of my time and energy the last six years to communicating those understandings to a wide audience. A great second order part of “teaching” is the opportunity for mastery it provides, which allows me to continue to “do” by managing client money and my own wealth. But that’s not my mission. My mission is to teach and explain.

I communicate via

* many free channels like dampedspring on twitter and here on substack but also in podcasts and television interviews
* [dampedspring.com](http://dampedspring.com) paid research

**The question I am often asked is if my mission is to pay forward my understandings and I don’t need the money why do I charge?**

My answer is pretty simple

* The education and information I provide to paying clients is worth multiples of what I charge
* I need to gate access to me such that only those willing to make a modest commitment to improving their understanding can demand time from me. I wish I had time to provide all of those with even the slightest bit of interest in my work full access, but I have found that I cannot possibly do that and sorting trolls from genunine interested parties is impossible without some financial gate. Basically a free resource gets abused and is allocated poorly.

Dampedspring.com was founded to pay forward my understanding of Macro to a wide audience. In 2026 I will be expanding the offering. I won’t be charging more. I won’t be offering multiple products to maximize my revenues. I will simply be increasing the scope of my existing product. For the last 16 years I have been focused on Macro investing. Prior to 2010 I spent 24 years paying attention to other things. It’s time to pay forward understandings that I have gained in the topics I spent most of my career pursuing.

DSRead, DSAlpha and DSInstitutional will continue to primarily focus on paying forward my understandings of macro but I will add DSStocks to mix.

The DSStocks effort will be focused on my experiences and understandings gained over the past 4 decades. But it won’t be a stock picking product. The one thing I have learned that I am as close to certain about is long/short stock picking with any sort of medium or long term horizon is something I cannot do well. Instead I will focus on “event driven” stock trading. This is an area that I have a better chance of having a competitive advantage. That advantage comes from experience in doing exactly this sort of investing for most of my life AND a perspective that I have gained over the more recent past of how macro conditions interact with single stock trading.

DSStocks will look for stock, credit, and option related trades based on known or potential events. The trades will likely fall in these various strategies and the idea is to formalize what has been an ad hoc sort of experience for DS Members so far.

* Inelastic flow due to a rebalance or inclusion or deletion (most recently was the FIX inclusion)
* Merger arbitrage (members participated in the U.S. Steel deal)
* Capital structure arbitrage (the MSTR/BTC arbitrage is a recent example) as was the signal to short the spike in ORCL due to the massive debt issuance required to fund data center build out
* Issuance related signals. (The BMNR, and IONQ issuance was a clear and exploitable short signal for those stocks)
* Concentrated pinning action of large option exposures and gamma squeezes and pins on single names. (JHEQX and QYLD)
* Insider selling (When Elon Musk signaled his need to sell shares to pay taxes we had that situation nailed on how to respond)
* We will also focus on large forced selling or buying of sectors or stocks based on liquidations or tax loss realization (We called the low in MAG7 in 2022)
* This list is not meant to be exhaustive but I won’t be providing ideas based on pure fundamentals of a company or a sector.

**More broadly if a stock or stock sector has a large price change due to things that are not directly related to the company’s business results or outlook but have more to do with flow, positioning, issuance, third party transacation, and the interaction with credit and options markets I will likely care about that situation.**

My goal is not to participate in all situations in all these categories as a business line. I don’t intend to trade every merger deal or every rebalance. I will cherry pick situations in which my specific set of understandings and experiences gives me an edge. I think we can make money and will provide specific trades but it could be weeks or months between trades. My goal is to help clients understand how these sort of situations work so that they can use that to better invest themselves.

I have extremely deep and broad experience in these topics and look forward to paying that knowledge forward. As part of DS this effort will be a pure add without detracting from the core mission of DampedSpring which will remain focused on macro. If you are interested in learning more about macro investing and special situation equity trading I think you will find the subscription well worth your money. If you want day trading advice I am not for you.

**In the end my mission is to get to know you and your interests in learning and meet you on your personal learning curve and substantially advance your progress on that learning curve. While many of my clients are professionals who lurk others are active in furthering their understanding. I think I can help you and I am committed to do so.**
