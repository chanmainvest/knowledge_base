## Description

In this presentation, Jeffrey Christian of CPM Group discusses the sharp pullback in gold and silver prices following weeks of rapid gains and growing talk of a “debasement trade.”

He explains why the recent decline in gold and silver does not signal a reversal of the longer-term upward trend and what investors should realistically expect in the near term. Jeff also looks at the fundamentals behind the U.S. dollar, showing that the dollar remains strong, widely held by central banks, and far from collapse.
