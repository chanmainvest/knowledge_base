## Description

In this presentation, Jeffrey Christian of CPM Group introduces Part 1 of the Silver Facts and Fantasies online seminar, addressing many of the questions investors have about silver prices, market fundamentals, and the narratives that frequently circulate in the precious metals market.

The discussion begins with an overview of CPM Group’s work in the silver market and the methodologies used to evaluate prices. Jeff explains how CPM Group combines macroeconomic analysis with detailed fundamental research on mine production, secondary recovery, fabrication demand, investment demand, inventories, and broader market developments. 

Jeff discusses key distinctions between facts and narratives in the silver market. He addresses the commonly discussed idea of $100 silver, explaining how commodity market fundamentals respond to higher prices through increased supply, recycling, profit taking, and changes in demand. The segment also discusses the role of investment demand and financial sentiment in shaping prices, highlighting why silver behaves both as an industrial commodity and as a financial asset held by investors around the world.
