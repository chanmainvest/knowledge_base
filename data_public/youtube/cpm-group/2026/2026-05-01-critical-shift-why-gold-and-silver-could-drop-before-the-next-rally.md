## Description

In this presentation, Jeffrey Christian of CPM Group discusses the evolving dynamics in gold, silver, platinum, and palladium markets. He explains how investors are becoming more comfortable with near-term economic conditions, even as underlying risks continue to build, creating a shift in investment behavior across different regions.

Jeff looks at how precious metals prices have transitioned from a strong rally into a period of consolidation, with the potential for sideways movement and intermittent weakness over the coming months. He discusses declining investment demand in the US alongside stronger buying interest in China and continued holding behavior in India
