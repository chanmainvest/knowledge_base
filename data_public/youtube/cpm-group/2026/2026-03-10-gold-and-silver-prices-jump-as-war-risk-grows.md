## Description

In this presentation, Jeffrey Christian of CPM Group discusses recent developments in gold and silver markets, focusing on the recent rise in prices and the broader implications of political tensions for investors.

Jeff looks at the impact of the escalating conflict involving Iran and its implications for global energy supplies and financial markets. He discusses how how the conflict may influence inflation, economic growth, and investment demand for safe-haven assets such as gold and silver. 

Jeff also explains why precious metals prices have not surged more dramatically as some may have expected.

The presentation concludes with a look at some of the  history of silver conspiracy theories that circulated during the 1990s, comparing them with similar narratives that continue to appear in the market today.
