## Description

In this presentation, Jeffrey Christian of CPM Group reviews the latest developments in gold, silver, platinum, and palladium markets, explaining why prices are rising again and what may come next. He discusses the recent price movements in gold and silver, including the sharp rally earlier in the year, the pullback following Federal Reserve policy signals, and the renewed strength tied to political tensions and broader concerns about global economic stability.

Jeff also addresses recent commentary around Russian gold sales, clarifying how the Russian central bank has been managing its reserves since the start of the Ukraine conflict and why recent sales are not unusual in that context. He then turns to the growing interest in hedging, particularly among mining companies, and explains how producers and investors can protect profits while maintaining upside exposure.
