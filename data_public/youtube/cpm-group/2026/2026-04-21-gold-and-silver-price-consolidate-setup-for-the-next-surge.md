## Description

In this presentation, Jeffrey Christian of CPM Group discusses the current consolidation phase across gold, silver, platinum, and palladium markets, and the unusually high level of uncertainty driving price volatility. 

He explains why gold prices may trade in a wide range over the coming months, reflecting shifting expectations around political developments, monetary policy, and broader economic conditions.

Jeff then discusses the growing spread of misinformation and flawed data in precious metals markets. He explains how commonly cited deficit figures and bullion banking assumptions are often misunderstood or incorrectly calculated, and why investors should be cautious when interpreting widely circulated "statistics".
