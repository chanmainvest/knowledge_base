## Description

In this presentation, Jeffrey Christian of CPM Group discusses the current consolidation phase in gold, silver, platinum, and palladium markets and explains why recent price weakness does not necessarily signal the end of the broader precious metals bull market. 

Jeff looks at the technical and fundamental factors driving gold and silver prices, including support levels, investor psychology, shifting economic expectations, and the possibility of a prolonged sideways trading period through the summer months before a continued upward move later this year. 

The video concludes with a discussion on the proposed “Silver Act” introduced in the U.S. House of Representatives and what it could mean for COMEX silver depositories, market logistics, and precious metals trading infrastructure in the United States.
