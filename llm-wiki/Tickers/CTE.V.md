A skeptic would point out that all of this is process rather than outcome — bank engagements, not closed commitments — and that a running figure around $10 million sits awkwardly beside a nine-figure debt raise, making the whole structure contingent on those asset-level marks arriving at the values implied. But no such skeptic appears anywhere in the record.

The timeline is correspondingly flat: nothing in the digest predates the August 19, 2026 remarks, nobody has flipped in either direction, and consensus hasn't budged from neutral/watch. The honest read is that the debate hasn't started yet. What exists is a single management thread — asset-level marks will reveal holdco value, project debt is being lined up — standing unopposed only because the other side hasn't shown up. Until an analyst is willing to rate the NAV story or the funding gap, this page remains a record of one voice talking to eight shrugs.
