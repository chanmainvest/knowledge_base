Yet the same voice leans contrarian on direction, arguing that "when everyone gets louder and more bearish right into those areas is historically when the setup for the catch up trade fires." It's a deliberately awkward position: the internals say the rally lacks fuel, the positioning says the fuel is precisely the crowd's bearishness. Hold both, and you get neutral/watch.

That convergence is the real shape of the debate here: one voice waits for confirmation of strength, the other waits for a washout, and neither is buying at current levels, but nobody in this slice is calling QQQ a straight short either.
