That sits awkwardly against a consensus row showing zero bullish calls, and it reads more like a level-based lean than a conviction call. The honest summary: nobody in this corpus is actually fighting about NQ. One anonymous map-maker has defined 29,250–30,000 as the battleground, the crowd is standing pat, and the lone "bullish" tag deserves skepticism until it's backed by a name and a firmer quote.
