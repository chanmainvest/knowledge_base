## The debate

There isn't much of a debate on CTH.V yet — the record shows thirteen neutral stances and not a single bull or bear on either side of the ledger. What exists instead is a management monologue, almost entirely from one date: August 19, 2026, when [Julian (CoTec CEO)](../People/julian-cotec-ceo.md) laid out a sprawling bull case in his own words. The page below is therefore less a tally of arguments than a snapshot of a pitch that outside voices haven't yet endorsed, challenged, or even engaged with.

The bull case, as Julian tells it, rests on scale and margin. He claims the company owns "17% of what could be the biggest iron ore deposit in North America," with "2.6 billion tons in the ground at 35%" [Julian (CoTec CEO)](../People/julian-cotec-ceo.md). Layered on top are a low-capex tailings angle — "around 100 million tons of tailings" with studies suggesting "about 70 million" to construct — and pig iron economics he framed as a production cost of "roughly 300 to 350" against a pig iron price of 500 and up [Julian (CoTec CEO)](../People/julian-cotec-ceo.md). He promised near-term validation, saying "we will be announcing in the next few days the qualities of the pellets that we have achieved," and sketched a vertical-integration play into magnets built in Europe and finished "in the Dallas Fort Worth" area in the first half of next year, plus a joint venture with "copper intelligence," described as a big copper developer in the DRC [Julian (CoTec CEO)](../People/julian-cotec-ceo.md).

The bear side hasn't spoken yet, but the sharpest cautionary material in the record comes from Julian himself. On valuation and funding, he conceded the market won't cooperate with the company's own number: "We obviously aren't going to be raising funding at the 2.5 billion pre-tax valuation. It will be a significant discount to that" [Julian (CoTec CEO)](../People/julian-cotec-ceo.md). And the capital plan is explicit — "we need $350 million, 200 of debt, 150 of equity" to reformat the concentrator and bring the plant into production — which implies real dilution against a stock with no analyst sponsorship [Julian (CoTec CEO)](../People/julian-cotec-ceo.md). The breadth of the pitch is itself a fair point of skepticism: pig iron, tailings, magnets, and a DRC copper JV are all being advanced at once, and every headline number comes from the sell-side of the same transaction.

On timeline and mind-changes, there's nothing to chart: every call in the corpus sits on that single August 19 date, some attributed to Julian and some logged under an unnamed speaker on the same appearance, with no prior or subsequent positions to compare against. Nobody has flipped bullish, nobody has turned bearish, and no one has been proven right or wrong yet. The near-term tests are the ones management set itself — the promised pellet-quality announcement and the actual terms of a raise Julian already admits will come at a discount to the $2.5 billion figure. Until outside analysts take positions, "neutral/watch" is less a verdict on the asset than an accurate description of a debate that hasn't started.
