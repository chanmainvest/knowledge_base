The June top is the shared anchor: Berg treats it as confirmation of a completed topping process, while the long-positioned speaker treats it as context for why the index's failure at the old gap highs matters. That's the shape of the debate, such as it is — thin coverage, one hard bear, one conflicted bull, and six neutrals whose silence is arguably the most telling data point in the file.
