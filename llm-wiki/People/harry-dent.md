**Role:** Economic forecaster, financial newsletter author, and investment strategist

Harry Dent is an economic forecaster, financial newsletter author, and investment strategist — a name long associated with loud, cycle-based crash forecasting. Everything below comes from a single dated appearance, an August 18, 2026 discussion of the "Everything Bubble," so treat this as a snapshot of his argument rather than a track record you can grade.

The snapshot is nonetheless coherent, because both positions hang off the same hook: a major crash. On equities, Dent's advice is to wait for the bust rather than buy ahead of it — the market is "going to be on sale for the best price," and once it is, "you want to buy things like QQQ, the NASDAQ 100, that captures the raciest side" ([Harry Dent on the Everything Bubble — And the Only Safe Hav…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TInyOyKDOgI)). The bond leg completes the trade: "buy the TLT or the 30-year Treasury bond and get a little higher interest and potential for a doubling or more if there is a major crash" ([Harry Dent on the Everything Bubble — And the Only Safe Hav…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TInyOyKDOgI)). In other words, QQQ is the post-crash entry and TLT is the crash itself paying you.

Note the shape of that, because it's characteristically Dent: neither call is a direction bet on today's tape, which is presumably why both are coded neutral. One is an entry plan gated on a downturn he expects; the other is an explicit crash hedge with leverage-like upside ("a doubling or more") if the downturn is severe. As for how his views have moved over time — the digest can't say. One date, one source, two conditional picks.
