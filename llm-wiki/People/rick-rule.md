# Rick Rule

**Role:** Natural resource investor; former President & CEO of Sprott U.S. Holdings; founder of Global Resource Investments; currently heads Rule Investment Media

Rick Rule is one of the most recognizable names in natural resource investing: he founded Global Resource Investments, served as President & CEO of Sprott U.S. Holdings, and now runs his own shop under Rule Investment Media. Over decades he built his reputation as a contrarian buyer of unloved resource assets — gold, silver, uranium, and the small-cap mining equities most institutions won't touch.

The record in this corpus comes almost entirely from a single late-August 2026 appearance, "[Why Rick Rule Is Buying Gold Now: Monetary Shock Incoming](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYQw_7Myw2g)," and it captures his thesis in miniature. He frames gold not as a chart trade but as a hedge against monetary debasement, arguing the US government is trying to drive down bond yields while continuing what he bluntly calls a "program of counterfeiting" — a word he pauses mid-sentence to qualify, suggesting he knows exactly how provocative it is ([Why Rick Rule Is Buying Gold Now: Monetary Shock Incoming](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYQw_7Myw2g)). His Treasury math backs the point: on his working assumptions, a genuinely riskless counterparty should demand roughly 150 basis points over the real yield, a framework for what long rates *ought* to be rather than a prediction of where they're headed ([Why Rick Rule Is Buying Gold Now: Monetary Shock Incoming](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYQw_7Myw2g)).

The genuinely interesting wrinkle is that despite the buy-now headline, Rule is openly nervous about the tape. "Whenever I see a momentum-driven chart... I get concerned for the very, very near term," he says — and he's beginning to see exactly that in gold ([Why Rick Rule Is Buying Gold Now: Monetary Shock Incoming](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYQw_7Myw2g)). Read carefully, the position is a two-clock trade: secular conviction in the monetary-shock thesis paired with near-term caution about parabolic price action. The all-neutral stance coding across this digest fits that tension better than any clean bull-or-bear label would.

A plain caveat: that one item, dated 2026-08-26, is essentially the entire record here. Rule's career clearly spans decades of resource-cycle calls, but this corpus contains no earlier entries and no documented shifts — so any claim that he "turned" bullish or bearish at a particular moment would be invention rather than evidence.
