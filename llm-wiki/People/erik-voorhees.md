Erik Voorhees is an American cryptocurrency entrepreneur and one of Bitcoin's earliest adopters, in the industry since roughly 2011 with a stint at BitInstant in its first chapter. He built the bitcoin betting site SatoshiDice and sold it in 2013, co-founded Coinapult, and is best known for launching the digital asset exchange ShapeShift in 2014, which he ran as CEO through its 2021 decentralization into a DAO before stepping away from day-to-day leadership; more recently he co-founded the privacy-focused AI platform Venice AI. The through-line is ideological: a committed libertarian who argues for the "separation of money and state," he tends to reason about markets as a monetary theorist with a portfolio rather than as a chart-watcher.

His corpus presence, it should be said up front, is thin — a single appearance on Raoul Pal's *The Journey Man* (June 18, 2026), in an episode framed around AI, Bitcoin and self-sovereignty. Within that one conversation, though, his strongest call is unambiguous: Bitcoin is "the best form of money that humans have ever created," and in his framing mass adoption follows from that premise almost as a matter of course ([AI, Bitcoin & Self-Sovereignty | Raoul Pal The Journey Man …](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nCpeLLNxl-g)). The conviction extends to the DeFi stack — "the DeFi innovations, lending markets... those tokens deserve to thrive more than they are" — and on whether they will, his answer was flatly "They should and will, but they haven't yet" ([AI, Bitcoin & Self-Sovereignty | Raoul Pal The Journey Man …](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nCpeLLNxl-g)). Notably, his explanation for the memecoin era is structural rather than moralistic: the industry "went through this period where everyone was just creating memecoins," he argues, because securities regulation made designing serious tokens too legally dangerous — a "damaging effect of securities regulation" in his telling ([AI, Bitcoin & Self-Sovereignty | Raoul Pal The Journey Man …](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nCpeLLNxl-g)).
