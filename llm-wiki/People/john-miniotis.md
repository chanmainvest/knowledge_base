([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)). That is a promoter interview, not a series of directional calls, so there is no view migration to trace here; what the record captures is the shape of the bull case as management tells it.

And the case is specific. His key asset is Diablillos, which he describes as "one of the largest, fully permitted, primary silver projects in the world," with "very robust economics" in a jurisdiction that has "improved dramatically" ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)). The recent study, he stresses, was run at deliberately conservative prices — $50 an ounce for silver and $3,650 for gold long-term, "well below where we're sitting today" ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)). His boldest superlative: in year three, "the Yellowhead would actually be the largest primary silver mine globally," with silver counting for more than 50% of revenue ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)).

The timeline is the operative part of the pitch. Construction activities begin next year — April 2027, per his own framing — on a roughly two-and-a-half-year build to commercial production in Q4 2029 ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)). He called the project "clearly very, very financeable," said active conversations were underway "across all three" of the avenues under discussion, and promised a steady stream of news flow from the current exploration program through year-end and into next year ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)). The thesis underneath: break ground and the company re-rates "into that developer category" rather than trading as an explorer ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)).

The honest caveat: one interview, one date, all management framing. Nothing in this corpus shows Miniotis making independent calls on gold or silver; the 13-of-13 neutral log reflects descriptive project updates, not studied conviction.
