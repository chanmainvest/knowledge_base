# John Miniotis

**interview guest (1x)**

**Role:** President, Chief Executive Officer & Director

John Miniotis is the President, Chief Executive Officer and a Director of Abra Silver (ABRA.V), and his public footprint in this corpus is, so far, exactly one appearance: a long-form interview with Jimmy Connor published August 14, 2026, framed around the host's question of whether there's "More To Come" after the stock's 45% year-to-date run ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)). That single-source record shapes how to read him. All thirteen extracted statements classify as neutral — no surprise for a founder-type executive walking through his own project. He makes no market call here on gold or silver outright, even though both metals underwrite his entire story.

The pitch centers on Diablillos, which he describes as "one of the largest, fully permitted… primary silver projects in the world," with robust economics "in a jurisdiction that's improved dramat[ically]" — Argentina, on the widely known geography ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)). The economics are built on deliberately conservative assumptions: the study used "$50 for silver long term, $3,650 for gold," which he notes is "of course well below where we're sitting today" ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)). That's the standard developer argument — bankable at bear-case prices, leveraged to anything better — and his optimism lives in the asset claims and the price-deck gap rather than in any forecast, which is why the transcript reads neutral.

The timeline he laid out in August 2026: a "steady stream of news flow" from the exploration program through year-end and into next year ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)); construction activities beginning next year, with "April of 2027" the date he gave ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)); and a roughly two-and-a-half-year build to "Q4 2029 when we hit commercial production" ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)). On financing — the perennial junior-developer choke point — he reported active conversations across three options and called the project "very, very financeable" ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)). The re-rating thesis is the kicker: once construction starts, he expects the market to move Abra Silver "into that developer category" ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)).

The second act is Yellowhead, which he claims would, in year three, be "the largest primary silver mine globally," with silver accounting for more than 50% of revenue ([Abra Silver Up 45% YTD - More To Come?  | John Miniotis and…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RbfUZb2Z2c)). Take the enthusiasm with the obvious grain of salt — this is a seller of the story, on a show whose own title does some of the hyping. And because the record is one interview on one date, there's no view drift to chart here; the only moving timeline is his construction schedule. Whether April 2027, the financing close-out he hinted at, and Q4 2029 hold is precisely what future coverage should test him on.
