The S&P framing is the guest's own — SpaceX is privately held — but that is the claim as recorded, and it is the one position that never wavered.

The rest of the book is quieter: bullish crude ("I'm actually I'm with you guys," deferring to the hosts on oil), and two outright holds — "I'm keeping Brazil" and "I'm keeping energy" ([George Noble On Stocks, Gold, Oil, and AI @nobull-markettal…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LDrOV4i0EU4)).
