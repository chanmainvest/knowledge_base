David Woo is a macro strategist and former Wall Street economist who spent roughly a decade at Bank of America Merrill Lynch as a managing director and head of global rates, currencies and emerging-markets research, after earlier stints at Barclays. He built a reputation as one of the sell side's more contrarian voices — he was among the first on Wall Street to publish research on bitcoin valuation, and he wrote regularly on currency wars, oil and geopolitics — before leaving the sell side around 2019 to go independent. He now runs the newsletter and interview series *David Woo Unbound* and shows up frequently as a podcast guest on macro, energy and markets, including on the Monetary Matters Network.
