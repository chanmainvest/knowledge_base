He made his name with outspoken, contrarian calls — including some of the earliest sell-side research on bitcoin valuation, plus commentary on currency wars, oil and geopolitics — before leaving the sell side around 2019 to work as an independent commentator. He now publishes the newsletter and interview series "David Woo Unbound" and is a fixture on finance podcasts; the record here comes from two 2026 appearances on The Monetary Matters Network, and it leans distinctly negative — 22 bearish stances against 13 bullish and 13 neutral.
