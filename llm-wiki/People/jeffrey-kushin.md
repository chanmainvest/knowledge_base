Jeffrey Kushin is a consultant and market analyst at CPM Group, the New York-based commodities research and consulting firm founded by Jeffrey Christian that has made precious metals its specialty. He works across the firm's research, consulting, and commentary on gold, silver, platinum, and palladium, and fronts CPM's video outlooks, where he translates the firm's supply-demand modeling into price forecasts ([Gold And Silver Market Update: How Low Before Buyers Return](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1jdQOS8Lu8)). Coverage here is thin — a single appearance in this corpus — so the portrait rests almost entirely on one outing.

That outing, a June 16, 2026 market update, shows a analyst who argues in ranges and time windows rather than slogans. On gold, he called for a "volatile sideways consolidation between now and September" with roughly $4,000 on the low end ([Gold And Silver Market Update: How Low Before Buyers Return](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1jdQOS8Lu8)), and said CPM expected the $4,100 level to be tested again over the following two and a half months, adding that "it may well hold" with further support at $3,800 and below ([Gold And Silver Market Update: How Low Before Buyers Return](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1jdQOS8Lu8)). The interesting part of the video is the split within precious metals. Platinum he treated bearishly, expecting a $1,600 to $2,000 trade over the next couple of months with a downside skew "because there's greater bearishness" in that market ([Gold And Silver Market Update: How Low Before Buyers Return](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1jdQOS8Lu8)), and palladium likewise as trading sideways "with a downward bend" as bullishness that emerged the prior June continued to bleed out ([Gold And Silver Market Update: How Low Before Buyers Return](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1jdQOS8Lu8)). Underneath the near-term chop, though, his framework is unabashedly fundamentalist: he leaned on CPM's long record, noting that when gold fell to $1,211 in 2014, "we said buy. The candor about being early is telling — this is a shop that would rather be directionally right on fundamentals than tactically punctual.
