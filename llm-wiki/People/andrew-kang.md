# Andrew Kang

**Role:** Cryptocurrency investor and venture capitalist — Founder and Partner, Mechanism Capital

Andrew Kang is a cryptocurrency investor and venture capitalist, best known as the founder and partner at Mechanism Capital — a firm that made its name in crypto markets. Yet the record captured here paints a picture of someone actively looking past his home turf. In an interview from August 20, 2026, titled "The Next Breakout Investing Opportunity? w/ Andrew Kang | R…," Kang lays out a thesis that is striking precisely because of who is making it: a Bitcoin-friendly crypto native arguing that the bigger prize lies elsewhere. "I think the AI total of all AI market cap is going to be at least 10, 20, 30x times larger than Bitcoin," he says, before immediately conceding, "Even though I'm, you know, I like Bitcoin" ([The Next Breakout Investing Opportunity? w/ Andrew Kang | R…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qsUh1sMDfs)).

His supporting evidence leans on the speed of private-market value creation. Kang points to OpenAI and Anthropic, which "in the span of three years just speedrun to a trillion dollars" — albeit, he notes, in private capital markets rather than public ones ([The Next Breakout Investing Opportunity? w/ Andrew Kang | R…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qsUh1sMDfs)). The most revealing moment, though, is structural: he benchmarks a prospective vehicle against MicroStrategy, observing that "their TAM was Bitcoin" and that the company "at their peak, they reached around a hundred billion dollars in market cap," before pivoting to a larger claimed TAM ([The Next Breakout Investing Opportunity? w/ Andrew Kang | R…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qsUh1sMDfs)). In other words, he appears to be recycling the Bitcoin treasury-company playbook for a new theme — with robotics as the stated destination: "we will reinvest... the goal is to be one of the largest robotics investors in the world," with an aim to increase robotics equity exposure ([The Next Breakout Investing Opportunity? w/ Andrew Kang | R…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qsUh1sMDfs)). He also invokes the serial-acquirer model — companies like Constellation Software or TransDigm "where their whole business model is based on acquisitions" — trading around 15 times, as a reference point for how such compounding vehicles get valued ([The Next Breakout Investing Opportunity? w/ Andrew Kang | R…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qsUh1sMDfs)).

What the snapshot does show, clearly, is the shape of the argument: a crypto VC using Bitcoin's own history as both measuring stick and template, sizing AI at 10–30x Bitcoin while attempting to port the MicroStrategy structure into robotics.
