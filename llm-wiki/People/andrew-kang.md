# Andrew Kang

**solo author/host (1x)**

**Role:** Cryptocurrency investor and venture capitalist — Founder and Partner, Mechanism Capital

Andrew Kang is a cryptocurrency investor and venture capitalist, best known as the founder and partner at Mechanism Capital, a crypto-focused investment firm. In this corpus he appears through a single long-form conversation — "The Next Breakout Investing Opportunity?" on Raoul Pal's *The Journey Man*, dated August 20, 2026 ([The Next Breakout Investing Opportunity? w/ Andrew Kang | R…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qsUh1sMDfs)) — and what's striking about it is how far beyond crypto the discussion ranges for someone whose career was built there.

His headline claim from that appearance: the total AI market cap is going to be "at least 10, 20, 30x times larger than Bitcoin," a statement he makes while explicitly volunteering that he still likes Bitcoin ([The Next Breakout Investing Opportunity? w/ Andrew Kang | R…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qsUh1sMDfs)). He grounds the scale argument in private markets rather than public tape, pointing to how OpenAI and Anthropic "in the span of three years just speedrun to a trillion dollars" in private valuations ([The Next Breakout Investing Opportunity? w/ Andrew Kang | R…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qsUh1sMDfs)).

The most concrete agenda item is robotics. Kang states plainly that "the goal is to be one of the largest robotics investors in the world," with reinvestment aimed at increasing robotics equity exposure ([The Next Breakout Investing Opportunity? w/ Andrew Kang | R…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qsUh1sMDfs)). He frames it using an explicitly borrowed template: "Micro Strategy, their TAM was Bitcoin. At their peak, they reached around a hundred billion dollars in market cap. Our TAM is the… market cap" ([The Next Breakout Investing Opportunity? w/ Andrew Kang | R…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qsUh1sMDfs)). He also reaches outside crypto for a compounding model, citing companies like Constellation Software and TransDigm "where their whole business model is based on acquisitions" ([The Next Breakout Investing Opportunity? w/ Andrew Kang | R…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qsUh1sMDfs)) — the vocabulary of a traditional equity operator applied to a robotics thesis.

And because it is a single snapshot from one date, there is no timeline of view movement to chart here. What the coverage does show, thin as it is, is the shape of an interesting argument: a crypto-native investor conceding his home market's ceiling while mapping the MicroStrategy playbook onto a different, larger TAM.
