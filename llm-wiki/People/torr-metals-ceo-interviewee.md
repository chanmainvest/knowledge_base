**Role:** Chief Executive Officer (President & CEO), Torr Metals Inc.

The chief executive and president of Torr Metals Inc. (TMET.V) makes only one documented appearance in this corpus: a Resource Talks interview dated August 10, 2026, titled "Copper-Gold Target in BC, But Will the New Targeting Deliver?" ([Copper-Gold Target in BC, But Will the New Targeting Delive…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZoqBYO-SWw)). That single conversation is the entire public record here, so the portrait has to be drawn from one long-form pitch rather than a track record of calls — and it should be read that way.

What he argues for is specific and geological. The core of the pitch is Bertha North, a copper-gold target in British Columbia that he frames as a New Afton-style system, with biotite-dominant alteration as the fingerprint he's chasing ([Copper-Gold Target in BC, But Will the New Targeting Delive…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZoqBYO-SWw)). His strongest claim of the day was that the very first drill hole "hit the type of zone that we were interpreting," which he said "speaks to multitudes" and, in his telling, de-risks the project ([Copper-Gold Target in BC, But Will the New Targeting Delive…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZoqBYO-SWw)). He ties the geology to a bigger prize, arguing these are "the types of structures that have the potential to host significant gold systems" ([Copper-Gold Target in BC, But Will the New Targeting Delive…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZoqBYO-SWw)).

The most concrete commitment was on the drill budget: he argued that the 6,000-meter program "would certainly be enough to demonstrate that" if dedicated entirely to Bertha North ([Copper-Gold Target in BC, But Will the New Targeting Delive…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZoqBYO-SWw)), with the first two holes designed to test and confirm the northwest orientation ([Copper-Gold Target in BC, But Will the New Targeting Delive…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZoqBYO-SWw)). Yet the same interview shows the promoter's hand on the brake: "ultimately that will come down to laboratory assays" ([Copper-Gold Target in BC, But Will the New Targeting Delive…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZoqBYO-SWw)).

On whether his views have moved over time — there is nothing to measure. The stance ledger reads six neutral entries, zero bullish, zero bearish, all from a single date; he makes no market-direction calls at all. The interesting tension is internal rather than temporal: de-risking language on hole one sits right alongside a refusal to claim anything ahead of assays. That's a coherent posture for a junior CEO — confident on geology, conditional on chemistry — but with one interview on file, the honest verdict is that the record is too thin to score him yet.
