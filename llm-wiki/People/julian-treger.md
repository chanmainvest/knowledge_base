# Julian Treger

**Role:** Natural resources financier and mining company executive

Julian Treger is a natural resources financier and mining company executive, best captured in this corpus as the voice of CoTec Holdings — theTSX-V-listed resources holding vehicle (CTH.V, with a related CTE.V entry) whose fortunes he was laying out in detail on the "Pitch Rick Ep #2" interview of August 19, 2026. He speaks in the register of an operator-promoter rather than an outside commentator: capital structures, hub-by-hub financing schedules, and spin-off mechanics, not directional market calls.

What he argues for, in that single appearance, is a value-unlock thesis built in stages. He described Mag Iron raising capital at a $550 million internal valuation via a rights issue, with CoTec holding 17% of it, and Hypermag being taken to market for funding on a hub-by-hub basis, with the first hub valued at "several hundred" million ([Pitch Rick Ep #2 - CoTec Holdings Corp.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i25DJxYcDGw)). The endgame he sketched is an IPO raising ideally $200 million of equity, spinning off the bulk of CoTec's stake to CoTec shareholders ([Pitch Rick Ep #2 - CoTec Holdings Corp.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i25DJxYcDGw)), backed by roughly $100–120 million of project finance debt being negotiated with two commercial banks and XM ([Pitch Rick Ep #2 - CoTec Holdings Corp.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i25DJxYcDGw)). On the operating side, he put the concentrator reformat and plant restart at $350 million — $200 million debt, $150 million equity — with domestic pig iron production costs of roughly $300–350 against a $500–550 pig iron price ([Pitch Rick Ep #2 - CoTec Holdings Corp.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i25DJxYcDGw)), and flagged an 18-month runway before revenues start at the end of the following year or the beginning of 2028 ([Pitch Rick Ep #2 - CoTec Holdings Corp.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i25DJxYcDGw)).

Notably, he tempers his own story. He pointed to the gap between a $2 million figure and roughly $100 million of NPV before beneficiation as an indication of the value he thinks is being missed ([Pitch Rick Ep #2 - CoTec Holdings Corp.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i25DJxYcDGw)), yet conceded outright that any raise won't come near the $2.5 billion pre-tax valuation — "a significant discount to that" ([Pitch Rick Ep #2 - CoTec Holdings Corp.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i25DJxYcDGw)). That mix of pitch and candor is the most distinctive thing the record shows about him.

On the question of how his views have moved: the digest simply doesn't show movement. Everything traces to the same August 2026 interview, all fifteen stance datapoints are neutral, and there are no bullish or bearish calls on record — his "strongest calls" are capital plans and timelines rather than ratings. Coverage this thin means any portrait of Treger as a market forecaster would be manufactured; what the corpus supports is a picture of a financier mid-pitch, walking through a multi-year funding and spin-off roadmap for CoTec's iron assets.
