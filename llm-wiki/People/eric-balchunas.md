# Eric Balchunas

**Role:** Senior ETF Analyst

Eric Balchunas is a Senior ETF Analyst, and one of the small handful of people whose names function as shorthand for the entire exchange-traded fund beat. His public profile is built at Bloomberg, where his core work is parsing fund launches, flows, fees and the structural quirks of the ETF wrapper, and amplified by an unusually large following for someone in a data-heavy research role — his running commentary on ETF flows has become one of the market's go-to real-time sentiment gauges. The clearest statement of his worldview came in book form with *The Bogle Effect* (2022): his through-line argument is that cheap, transparent, passively managed structures are still mid-conquest of the expensive active fund complex, and that Jack Bogle's fee war is the single most consequential force reshaping asset management. He is, in short, an evangelist for the low-cost index revolution — and a persistent skeptic of anything charging more to deliver what a broad ETF already provides.

That positioning makes him easy to summarize in broad strokes and surprisingly hard to score in a stance-shaped corpus. His daily output is descriptive — who launched what, how much money moved, which fee got cut — rather than the buy/sell verdicts that map cleanly onto bullish/bearish/neutral grids. He is famous for narrating events like the spot bitcoin ETF approval saga, but narrating a story is not the same as putting a rating on it.
