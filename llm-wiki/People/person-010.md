高天佑 is a Hong Kong–based financial journalist and columnist, best known for 新聞點評 ("News Commentary"), his column in the Hong Kong Economic Journal (信報) covering markets, economic policy and current affairs, after earlier stints on Apple Daily's finance pages; he also fronts the solo audio/podcast versions of his columns. In this corpus he appears mostly as a one-man show — eight solo authored/hosted pieces against two guest interviews — and the tone of those calls is unmistakably downbeat: of twenty-two recorded stances, fifteen are bearish, four bullish, three neutral. One caveat on the record: coverage clusters in a single stretch of June 2026, so any longer-run read on the man is provisional.

That June opens cheerfully. His strongest bullish call came on June 16, when he held up the "Hefei model" — a local cluster of semiconductor firms "riding the AI wave" — as a template Hong Kong's Northern Metropolis could borrow, and spotlighted ChangXin Technology (長鑫科技) as one of only four companies worldwide able to mass-produce HBM memory, earning RMB 33 billion in Q1 alone, which he rendered as "RMB 360 million a day" ([北都可借鑑「合肥模式」](https://www1.hkej.com/dailynews/finnews/article/4431990/%E5%8C%97%E9%83%BD%E5%8F%AF%E5%80%9F%E9%91%91%E3%80%8C%E5%90%88%E8%82%A5%E6%A8%A1%E5%BC%8F%E3%80%8D)). The next day he was scrupulously agnostic on the spikier question of speculative northbound money and "anti-capital-flight" policy — the fuss that spooked Centaline's Shih Wing-ching — laying out the two camps without picking a side ([北水炒風勁 嚇親施永青](https://www1.hkej.com/dailynews/finnews/article/4432968/%E5%8C%97%E6%B0%B4%E7%82%92%E9%A2%A8%E5%8B%81+%E5%9A%87%E8%A6%AA%E6%96%BD%E6%B0%B8%E9%9D%92)).

Then the tape turned him, in stages. On June 18 he argued that mainland e-commerce platforms' "618" offensive into Hong Kong would squeeze small and mid-sized local retailers who cannot match deep-pocketed mainland giants, with knock-on pressure on unemployment, shop vacancy, rents and property valuations — while conceding consumers would be the short-term winners ([618攻港 本地零售難捱](https://www1.hkej.com/dailynews/finnews/article/4434076/618%E6%94%BB%E6%B8%AF+%E6%9C%AC%E5%9C%B0%E9%9B%B6%E5%94%AE%E9%9B%A3%E6%8D%B1)). On June 23 came the labor-market version of the same pessimism: mainland employment deteriorating, workers over 35 treated as prime layoff candidates, JD.com no longer wanting its couriers ([京東不要快遞員 三項鐵人再培訓](https://www1.hkej.com/dailynews/finnews/article/4436952/%E4%BA%AC%E6%9D%B1%E4%B8%8D%E8%A6%81%E5%BF%AB%E9%81%9E%E5%93%A1+%E4%B8%89%E9%A0%85%E9%90%B5%E4%BA%BA%E5%86%8D%E5%9F%B9%E8%A8%93)). And on June 24, taking the Party journal Qiushi's warning as his news hook, he put a number on the property damage — national home prices down more than 30% since the 2021 developer blowup, forcing a rethink of the domestic-demand model ([《求是》響警號 內需新思維](https://www1.hkej.com/dailynews/finnews/article/4437891/%E3%80%8A%E6%B1%82%E6%98%AF%E3%80%8B%E9%9F%BF%E8%AD%A6%E8%99%9F+%E5%85%A7%E9%9C%80%E6%96%B0%E6%80%9D%E7%B6%AD)).

The capstone came on June 25, when his recorded equity stance — bullish the day before — flipped hard bearish as he narrated a global rout: US AI leaders down more than 10% in a single session, South Korea's market off 9.99%, Hong Kong at a one-year low. Read together, the week has a consistent shape: bullish on China's AI-industrial engine while the story was about profits (Hefei, ChangXin), bearish almost everywhere the same forces touched households — retail, jobs, property — and then bearish on the market itself once the AI trade broke. Whether that reads as nimbleness or whiplash, the nine days separating his celebration of an AI chipmaker's daily haul from his chronicle of the AI-stock crash is the defining tension of his recent record.
