# Emmanuel Macron

**Role:** President of France (2017–present); former investment banker at Rothschild & Cie

> Emmanuel Macron (born December 21, 1977, Amiens, France) is a French politician serving as President of France since 2017. Before politics, he had a career in finance: after training as an Inspecteur des finances in the French civil service, he joined the investment bank Rothschild & Cie Banque in 2008, becoming a partner and dealmaker. He is best known in finance circles for advising Nestlé on its roughly $12 billion acquisition of Pfizer's infant-nutrition business in 2012, a deal that reportedly earned him a large bonus and the press nickname 'the Mozart of finance.' He left banking to serve as deputy secretary-general of the Élysée Palace (2012–2014) and Minister of Economy, Industry and Digital Affairs (2014–2016) under President François Hollande. He founded the centrist En Marche! movement (now Renaissance) in 2016 and won the presidency in 2017, defeating Marine Le Pen; he was re-elected in 2022.
