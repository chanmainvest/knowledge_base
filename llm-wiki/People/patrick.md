The record here reflects that temperament precisely: fourteen neutral readings, not one outright bullish or bearish stance. That reads less like fence-sitting than method — his phrasing runs to "on balance of probabilities," gated scenarios and retracement math, and he openly polices his own hedging mid-sentence.

The cleanest window into his process is the S&P 500. On August 13, 2026, in the post-interview segment of episode #545 with Michael Howell, he laid out an upside trigger: a short-term catalyst breaking the market out of its one-week trade range to fresh highs could take it to 8,000 within a week — but only, he stressed, if the MAG7s were participating ([MacroVoices #545 Michael Howell: Warsh vs. The Markets](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xn3Mkp6FCss)). A week later on episode #546 with Darius Dale, with the market pulling back, the emphasis flipped from breakout mechanics to retracement tolerance: a pullback to 7,600 was "par for the course," a fifty-percent retrace "very typical," and he saw himself "far from seeing significant technical damage" on the index ([MacroVoices #546 Darius Dale: Darius Dale for POTUS 2028](https://www.macrovoices.com/1546-macrovoices-546-darius-dale-darius-dale-for-potus-2028)). Same neutrality, different posture — the shift from "what has to happen for 8,000" to "what pullback is still benign" is the tell of a level-watcher, not a closet bull or bear.
