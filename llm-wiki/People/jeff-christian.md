# Jeff Christian

**Role:** Managing Partner

Jeff Christian is a Managing Partner at CPM Group, the commodities research consultancy he's long been associated with, and in precious metals circles he occupies a particular niche: less a market cheerleader than a forecaster who will put a specific annual average and year-end range on gold, hedged but numerical. The record in this corpus is a snapshot of that style. Speaking on August 25, 2026, he laid out the firm's working numbers — an average gold forecast for the year of roughly $4,450, which he indicated would likely be revised upward from there ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7bgdCd8hJFw)).

His most concrete call from that appearance is the year-end target: gold in the $4,800 to $5,000 range, possibly "a little higher" ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7bgdCd8hJFw)). He layered tactical caution on top of the structural view, allowing for a pullback in early September while maintaining that the metal would rise from that dip into year-end ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7bgdCd8hJFw)). Read together, the substance is constructive — rising targets, higher revisions — even if his delivery is the measured tone of an analyst defending a model rather than talking a book.

Two caveats worth flagging. First, the corpus codes all three of these stances as neutral, and that's fair to the letter of his language: he rarely says "buy," he says what the price will average and where it will trade, which is a different rhetorical register than the bull/bear shouting match around him. Second, this coverage is thin — every entry comes from a single August 25, 2026 appearance, so there is no timeline here showing how his views have moved over the years. What it does show, clearly, is a forecaster at $4,450-and-rising averages with a $5,000 year-end bullseye, which is itself a position worth tracking against what follows.
