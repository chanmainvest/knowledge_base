Blue Macellari is a digital assets strategist, best known to macro audiences from her time running digital assets strategy at the sell-side firm BTIG, and in this corpus she appears exactly once: as a guest on Raoul Pal's *The Journey Man* in the August 13, 2026 episode "Why The Next Phase of Crypto Is Bigger Than You Think" ([Why The Next Phase of Crypto Is Bigger Than You Think w/ Bl…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q2TAHZIBpdE)). That single appearance is the entire record here, and it's worth stating plainly: six logged stances, all neutral, zero directional calls on any asset. She is diagnosing a regime, not picking tops or bottoms — which is itself characteristic of how sell-side digital-asset strategists tend to show up on macro podcasts.

Her headline argument from that conversation is about where crypto's marginal buyer went: "I think that for your marginal crypto buyer that AI sucked a lot of air out of the room" — the same risk-hungry retail dollar that would otherwise chase tokens is currently feeding the AI trade ([Why The Next Phase of Crypto Is Bigger Than You Think w/ Bl…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q2TAHZIBpdE)). She extended the diagnosis to crypto's narrative engine itself, arguing it became "impossible to sustain that type of constant transformational news flow" — the sector had conditioned itself to need perpetual revolution, and that pace ran out of road ([Why The Next Phase of Crypto Is Bigger Than You Think w/ Bl…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q2TAHZIBpdE)). It's a more structural explanation than the usual "AI took the liquidity" line: the crowding-out is real, but crypto's exhaustion was partly self-inflicted.

The constructive half of her thesis is about plumbing, not price. Different blockchains are suited to different things, she argued, which makes interoperability "absolutely key" — the endgame is a fully interoperable landscape rather than a single winning chain ([Why The Next Phase of Crypto Is Bigger Than You Think w/ Bl…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q2TAHZIBpdE)). Within that, she named the prize: the winner will be whoever can "abstract away the back end and bridging" — all the friction that crypto natives tolerate and everyone else won't ([Why The Next Phase of Crypto Is Bigger Than You Think w/ Bl…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q2TAHZIBpdE)). And she identified the next cohort of risk-takers with some conviction: "the marginal buyer of the SpaceX IPO is like a Solana holder. I absolutely think that" — in her read it's one retail animal rotating across speculative venues, whether that venue is a token or a pre-IPO ([Why The Next Phase of Crypto Is Bigger Than You Think w/ Bl…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q2TAHZIBpdE)).
