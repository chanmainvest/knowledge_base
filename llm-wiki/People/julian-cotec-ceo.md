Julian Treger runs CoTec Holdings Corp., a TSX Venture Exchange-listed investment company that bets on disruptive technologies across the mining and metals sector — and his profile fits the mandate. At CoTec his thesis in practice is that the money in mining increasingly sits in processing and production technology rather than in the ground: the company has backed green iron ore pellet and pig iron production initiatives, magnesium extraction through Magratea Metals, and cyanide-free gold leaching via EnviroLeach.

What makes the pitch interesting is the value math he volunteers.
