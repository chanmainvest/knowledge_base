George Gammon is an American real estate investor turned macroeconomic commentator, best known as the creator and host of The Rebel Capitalist Show, a popular YouTube channel and podcast. He cut his teeth fixing and flipping houses before pivoting to the plumbing of the global financial system — the repo market, the Eurodollar system, FX swaps, the Fed's balance sheet, and the role of the US dollar — which he unpacks in trademark whiteboard-style explainers that lean heavily on frameworks popularized by Jeff Snider, a frequent collaborator. A recurring guest on MacroVoices and Real Vision, Gammon has built his brand on skepticism toward official Fed and Treasury narratives, with a standing thesis that the global dollar system is far more fragile than the mainstream admits.
