# Doug (CEO, Strategic Metals)

**Role:** Chief Executive Officer

Doug runs Strategic Metals (SMD.V), and the honest way to describe his public posture is that of a portfolio-keeper rather than a promoter. The company's story, as he told it in an interview dated August 17, 2026, is one of scale without payoff: some 80 projects and 17 royalties, and the open question posed by that conversation's very title — can they actually be monetized? ([80 Projects and 17 Royalties, But Can They Really Monetize …](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M0eDetccI5M)). Doug's answer is patient but conditional. He argues the market will eventually recognize the company's value, but concedes it "is going to take one of a number of catalyst[s]" to get there ([80 Projects and 17 Royalties, But Can They Really Monetize …](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M0eDetccI5M)). That's a notably measured stance for a CEO — no date, no specific trigger, just a menu of possibilities.

Where he does get concrete is on execution and on the past. On the operational side, he points to a couple of years spent "getting our ducks in line" to move projects into play ([80 Projects and 17 Royalties, But Can They Really Monetize …](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M0eDetccI5M)). More striking is his candor on the backward-looking question: asked about the ten-year shareholder experience, he acknowledged flatly that "you would have been down," because a decade ago the company was still "on the shoulder of a very active exploration program" that has since receded ([80 Projects and 17 Royalties, But Can They Really Monetize …](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M0eDetccI5M)). It's a rare admission in this corner of the market, and it frames the debate well — the bull case here rests entirely on the unmonetized portfolio, not on any track record of delivery.

A note on limits: the record here is thin — a single interview, everything dated 2026-08-17, and all of it classified neutral, with no bullish or bearish calls on the tape.
