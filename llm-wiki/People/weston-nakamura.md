In other words, this isn't a dollar-strength story; it's a yen-specific one. And he is openly skeptical that the intervention successfully caps the pair for the long term ([The Real Story Behind the Yen Intervention | Weston Nakamur…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XFNk96CabCg)). The root cause, in his telling, is credibility at the Bank of Japan: he was scathing about Governor Ueda delivering "yet another horrendous performance" with no conviction behind his rate hike, arguing that a central bank not seen as credibly hiking in the face of inflation guarantees the spillover into yen weakness ([The Real Story Behind the Yen Intervention | Weston Nakamur…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XFNk96CabCg)).
