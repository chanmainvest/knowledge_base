# Matt (Radisson CEO)

**Role:** President and Chief Executive Officer

Matt — the record identifies him only by first name — is the president and chief executive officer of Radisson (TSXV: RDS.V), a Quebec-focused gold company, and his file in this corpus is refreshingly honest about being thin: every word on record comes from a single interview dated August 26, 2026, built around Agnico Eagle's $57 million investment in Radisson's Quebec gold project ([Agnico Invests $57M in Quebec Gold Project, But Is It Enoug…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bY40DOJPvxk)). That matters for how you read him. The corpus logs seven stances, all neutral, zero bullish, zero bearish — because what's on tape is a CEO explaining his own transaction, not a track record of market calls.

What he argues for is a resource-growth story. In the interview he walked the count up from "900,000 ounces of resources in 2023" to "a million and a half ounces last year. All categories," with the same remarks running the figure to 2.3 million ounces ([Agnico Invests $57M in Quebec Gold Project, But Is It Enoug…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bY40DOJPvxk)). On that trajectory he's "guided the market" toward a target of "3 to 4 million ounces" for the project, and his evidence of execution is a claimed drill success rate — "we're hitting 80% of the time" ([Agnico Invests $57M in Quebec Gold Project, But Is It Enoug…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bY40DOJPvxk)).

His strategic posture on the Agnico deal is the most substantive call on file: "We're selling 15% of the business here. I don't think we're ready to sell 100% of the business because this is a growing resource" ([Agnico Invests $57M in Quebec Gold Project, But Is It Enoug…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bY40DOJPvxk)). He paired that with a promise of cadence — resource updates "on a step-by-step basis," one already done "last March," and "a lot of drilling related news flow" in the pipeline ([Agnico Invests $57M in Quebec Gold Project, But Is It Enoug…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bY40DOJPvxk)) — plus some pointed flattery of the new shareholder: "there's no better gold mining company out there" ([Agnico Invests $57M in Quebec Gold Project, But Is It Enoug…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bY40DOJPvxk)). Read together, it's the classic minority-stake playbook: take the major's balance sheet and credibility, keep the exploration upside, and argue that a growing resource makes static deal math the wrong lens. Whether the $57M is "enough" — the very question in the interview's framing — he never really answers; he reframes it.

On movement of views over time: there is none observable, and it's worth saying plainly. All recorded quotes share one date, so any trend line here would be manufactured. The only through-line is the consistency of the message itself — target, cadence, partial sale — which is at least a coherent one.
