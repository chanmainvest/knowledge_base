Daniel Lacalle is a Spanish economist and former fund manager who has built his public profile on one consistent argument: that governments across the developed world are financing themselves with money that isn't theirs, and that central banks will keep debasing currencies rather than admit it. He is the author of books such as *The Energy World Is Flat* and *Freedom or Equality*, a well-known critic of MMT-style fiscal thinking and interventionist economics, and a recurring MacroVoices guest.
