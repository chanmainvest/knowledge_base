施永青 is one of those rare figures who sits on both sides of the property ledger — he sells it, writes about it, and opines on it. Born in Shanghai in 1949, he co-founded Centaline Property Agency (中原地產) in 1978 and built it into one of the largest real-estate agencies in Hong Kong and mainland China; in 2005 he added a second platform, founding the free Chinese-language newspaper am730, where his widely read columns range across property, economics and current affairs. Known for free-market views laced with Daoist philosophy, he is among Hong Kong's most quoted voices on housing, and his single recorded appearance in this corpus is as an interview guest opposite 高天佑.
