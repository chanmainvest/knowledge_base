That one appearance is nonetheless flavorful. Skousen's signature line from it is a piece of market fatalism with teeth: the stock market will "never commit suicide, but it can be murdered" — his argument being that markets don't kill themselves, bad fiscal policy does. He grounds that in the numbers, noting the United States is "approaching $40 trillion on our national debt" and framing the whole discussion around whether the Treasury can keep yields contained under that weight ([The Market’s $40T Question: Can Treasury Keep Yields Under …](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SWsGPoxqcR0)).
