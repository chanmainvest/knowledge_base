Jeff shows up in this corpus the way a lot of macro diagnosticians do: as a repeat interview guest on Eurodollar University — twice on the record — rather than as a headline name with his own book of calls. That matters for how you should read him. Eurodollar University's whole frame is the global dollar system, curves, and cross-market plumbing, and Jeff operates squarely inside it: he talks about Swiss two-year yields and Hang Seng reflation signals before he talks about any stock you'd recognize. The record here is also honest about its own limits — three appearances, all compressed into June 2026, spanning oil, global rates, and the late-cycle behavior of crypto and private credit. That's a thin window to build a portrait from, but the shape of his thinking is unusually consistent across it.

The clearest directional call came first. On June 14, in an episode framed around the aftermath of the Iran conflict ("Now That Iran Is Done, The Real Oil Crisis Begins"), Jeff reached for the old trader's adage — oil goes "up like a rocket, down like a feather" — and then did something more interesting than quote it: he rejected the comfortable assumption that prices simply round-trip to $50, insisting the eventual destination is a question of timing, not whether ([Now That Iran Is Done, The Real Oil Crisis Begins](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTh7cPI8z3E)). The corpus codes that stance bullish on energy, and it's worth noting he hedged and committed in the same breath — the rocket-and-feather framing right next to the firm call. That rhythm is the man.

A week later, on June 21, he widened the lens from oil to the global bond complex, and this is where his label gets counterintuitive. The episode is titled a "Dire Warning to the World," yet his coded stance is *bullish* — because what he's bullish on is duration. Swiss rates "almost back at zero all over again," the critical two-year yielding just a couple of basis points; Chinese rates "similarly ultra" with the curve bull steepening ([Swiss Bond Market Just Gave A Dire Warning to the World](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c2LzapdRuT4)). He draws the same split inside the curve itself: the front end goes up, while the long end is "saying, wait a minute" on growth and inflation expectations ([Swiss Bond Market Just Gave A Dire Warning to the World](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c2LzapdRuT4)). Translation: the warning is a bond bull's warning. When Jeff says dire, he's not calling for a crash — he's calling for rates to keep falling as the growth-inflation picture rolls over.

By June 27 he'd turned that plumbing-heavy method on the story everyone else was telling causally — Bitcoin and BlackRock moving in lockstep. His read refuses the narrative either way: "not Bitcoin leading to BlackRock or BlackRock leading to Bitcoin," but shared "late cycle stage behaviors" acting on both ([Bitcoin And BlackRock Just Did Something VERY Strange](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=biyCX7UYAfQ)). The evidence he reaches for is redemption mechanics, not price action: Apollo's fund gone cash-flow negative, redemption requests piling up and getting blocked ([Bitcoin And BlackRock Just Did Something VERY Strange](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=biyCX7UYAfQ)), investors wanting out of BlackRock's private credit complex and out of Bitcoin simultaneously ([Bitcoin And BlackRock Just Did Something VERY Strange](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=biyCX7UYAfQ)) — all while he keeps the Hang Seng in his pocket as a "pretty dependable" reflation gauge and openly doubts the mainstream "strong and resilient" read on the US economy ([Bitcoin And BlackRock Just Did Something VERY Strange](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=biyCX7UYAfQ)). Here's the honest verdict the stance mix shows: five bullish, zero bearish, fifteen neutral. Jeff is not a perma-bear and not a hype man — he's a diagnostician who will tell you the machine is grinding in a late-cycle way, name the exact basis points where it's showing, and then stop short of pointing at a short. If you want conviction, his is conditional; if you want someone who explains *why* Bitcoin and Apollo are doing the same thing at the same time, that's exactly what he's for.
